## Symbol Legend

- **D** = Downstroke ↓
- **U** = Upstroke ↑
- **X** = Mute/Chuck (percussive sound)

## Real Usage Flow

1. **Select your mode**: Jam, Practice, or Groove
2. **Choose settings**: 4/4, Intermediate, Major+Minor, 2 bars, 90 BPM
3. **Generate**: Click "Generate New"
4. **Preview**:
   ```
   Am        G
   D U X D U U D X U D U X D U
   ```
5. **Practice**: Click "Start Practice" - metronome syncs, current bar highlights
6. **Repeat**: Click "New Pattern" for instant variation

## Why This Works

Every pattern is:
- ✅ **Playable** - Natural hand movements
- ✅ **Musical** - Feels like real songs
- ✅ **Random** - Never repeats exactly
- ✅ **Progressive** - Difficulty appropriate
- ✅ **Clean** - No clutter, just D U X

---

## Practice Tips

1. **Start slow** - Begin at 60-80 BPM
2. **Master one pattern** - Use Practice Mode to drill chord changes
3. **Increase tempo gradually** - Add 5-10 BPM when comfortable
4. **Mix it up** - Switch to Jam Mode for variety
5. **Focus on timing** - Use the metronome sync
6. **Practice daily** - 10-15 minutes with the generator beats hours of aimless practice
